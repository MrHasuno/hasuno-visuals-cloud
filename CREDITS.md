Bauloo0, used and modified with permission. The fork's own changes are listed in the mod's README.

## The packs in this hub
Every style currently here was published by its author in the community library at
[Bauloo0/bauloo-styles-cloud](https://github.com/Bauloo0/bauloo-styles-cloud). The style ids, the
display names and the previews are the originals, kept so players can recognise them.

**Read this before publishing the hub.** Re-hosting other people's packs moves them off the
library they were published in and onto a repository under your name. That is fine only if:

- the pack's author is credited (the display names here are theirs),
- the pack's licence allows redistribution, and
- Bauloo's own terms for the library allow it.

If any of those is not true for a pack, delete it from `sources/`, run the rebuild, and it goes
away. Safer long term: fill the hub with packs you made, packs you have written permission for,
or packs under MIT / CC0 / CC-BY (with the credit).
